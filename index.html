HELPFUL TO KNOW
Before you begin.
Can a project start with an existing antibody?
Yes, that is a proposed entry route. A project could begin with an existing reagent, a new target or an assay that needs optimization. Suitability and availability would be reviewed for the intended application.
How will pricing work?
The proposed model uses published pack sizes and prices for approved catalog products, and scoped quotes for custom projects. Development quotes should identify milestones, deliverables, timeline and any optional work. No rates are approved or displayed in this concept.
What is the relationship with the Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory?
The Hub concept focuses on reagent products, technology development and research collaborations. Routine clinical specimen submissions and diagnostic testing should follow the existing Illinois VDL pathway. Any shared services or referral process would be agreed with the relevant teams.
Are all five service areas available now?
These five areas describe the proposed website scope. Service owners, project acceptance, available methods, product inventory and service-specific terms require confirmation before launch.
