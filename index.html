HELPFUL TO KNOW
Before you begin.
Can a project start with an existing antibody?
A project can begin with an existing reagent, a new target or an assay that needs optimization. Suitability and availability are reviewed for the intended application.
How will pricing work?
The mAb services page lists indicative project fees. A written quotation confirms the scope, milestones, deliverables and final charges, subject to applicable institutional rate approval.
What is the relationship with the Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory?
The Hub focuses on reagent products, technology development and research collaborations. Routine clinical specimen submissions and diagnostic testing should follow the existing Illinois VDL pathway. Any shared services or referral process would be agreed with the relevant teams.
Are all five service areas available now?
Service availability, project acceptance, available methods, product inventory and service-specific terms are confirmed with the relevant team before work begins.
